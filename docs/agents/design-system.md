## Gotchas

- **(Phase 44) "Ink and indigo" — two faces, no template chrome.** `--font-display` is Shippori Mincho, used ONLY to ask and answer: `h1`, `h2`, and the answer sentence (`.readout-headline`). `--font-body` is Instrument Sans for everything functional, including card `h3`/`h4` and every number; it ships `tnum` (verified against the font files before choosing; two other candidates lacked it). `--font-mono` (IBM Plex Mono) is reserved for code-like strings: the reproducibility chip, request ids, methodology code, and the drawer toggle glyph. There are no tracked all-caps labels (`text-transform: uppercase` count is 0). `.eyebrow` is a sentence-case muted label, used only when it adds information the heading does not ("Experimental", "Free, no LLM", "Current book"); redundant ones such as "Attribution" above "What drove the P&L" were deleted.
- **(Phase 44) One flat paper ground; chrome by hierarchy, not by habit.** Cards are `--panel-veil` + a hairline, radius 14px, with an effectively flat `--elevation-card`. Only the answer band is raised (radius 20px, `--elevation-answer`), and the readout and evidence sit flat inside it, divided by one hairline (top when stacked, left at ≥1600px). Rail panels are flat sections (`.rail .panel` / `.rail-drawer-panel .panel`: no border, no background, `border-radius: 0` — a leftover radius curls the ends of the access hairline), and access sits last. Controls use radius 10px; chips and pills use 999px.
- **(Phase 44) Indigo is the only brand colour, so it must stay meaningful.** `--accent` is the CTA fill, with text in `--on-accent` (white in light, ink in dark). `--accent-2` marks the active scenario chip, the scenario's chart marks, links, and the active area tab. Segmented controls and results tabs show their active option as a raised `--surface` pill, NOT an indigo fill. A segmented group with three or more options switches from the two-column grid to a wrapping flex row (`.segmented:not(.drivers-view):has(> :nth-child(3))`), so it sits on one line when there is room instead of splitting 2 + 1. Vermilion and pine are money only: negative factor EXPOSURES (book profile bars) render in `--chart-analog` muted ink, never `--down`.
- **(Phase 44) Compact 44px overrides must out-rank component rules.** `.results-tabs .tablist [role="tab"]` (the 40px pill) beats the generic compact `.tablist [role="tab"]` rule on specificity, so the ≤1079.98px 44px list names it explicitly. The release gate caught this at 320px. Any new component rule that sets a `min-height` below 44px needs the same entry.
- **(Phase 44) The theme hex lives in four places — keep them equal.** `--bg` in both token blocks, `frontend/index.html` (the `theme-color` meta and the boot script), `theme.ts` `THEME_COLORS` (pinned by `theme.test.ts`), and `public/manifest.webmanifest`. Night is `#0e1419`; Paper is `#f3f4f0`.
- **(Phase 44) The phone topbar keeps title and icon buttons on one row.** `.topbar-nav-cluster { flex: 1 1 0 }` at ≤640px lets the title wrap instead of claiming its one-line width; `overflow-wrap: anywhere` on the `h1` protects the 320px / 200%-text no-overflow contract. The ≥1080px topbar hides the portfolio chip because the inline rail already names the book.

- **(Phase 43) Chart purpose tokens: `--chart-range`, `--chart-stem`, `--chart-analog`, `--chart-segment-wash`**, each with a light override. Encoding is semantic: the scenario move is `--accent-2` (the world — neutral identity, never gain/loss), matched-event moves are hollow `--chart-analog` rings with a 2px surface ring on the filled marker, and effect bars are `--down`/`--up` with a 4px rounded data-end and a square end at zero. The validator measured the red/green pair at deutan ΔE 7.6 (dark) / 6.1 (light) — legal only WITH secondary encoding — so polarity must also ride the bar's direction from the zero line and the signed label. Never drop the sign or centre the bars.
- **(Phase 43) The Evidence block lays out by its OWN width.** `.evidence-block { container: evidence / inline-size }` and the stacked layout is `@container evidence (max-width: 40rem)`, not a viewport query: at ≥1600px the block occupies the 2fr track of the answer band, where the three-column row crushed the axis and collided tick labels. Range rows (`li.is-range`) put their two-number value on its own line in that mode (it used to overlap the label and spill past the card at 320px).
- **(Phase 43) Evidence rows share columns through `grid-template-columns: subgrid`.** Each `li` spans `.evidence-rows`' one template; giving each row its own template sizes the `auto` value column to that row's text and silently misaligns the "shared" axis (it shipped that way for one pass).
- **(Phase 43) `.results-toolbar` is a two-column grid** (actions `auto` | display `minmax(0, 1fr)`), and the reproducibility chip takes its own line inside the display group (`flex-basis: 100%`). As a flex row the display group's max-content width counted the chip, so the whole group wrapped under a stranded "Export all". Stacks to one column ≤640px.
- **(Phase 43) `.segmented.drivers-view`** uses three content-sized tracks with `white-space: nowrap`, and `.waterfall-card .card-heading-actions` does not shrink, so the heading copy wraps instead of the switch or the fullscreen button.
- **(Phase 43) `.readout-hero-value` is the page's single hero figure**: the body sans (never the display serif), proportional digits (no `tabular-nums`), `clamp(2.25rem, …, 3.25rem)`, colored by direction like the rest of the P&L surfaces.

- **Responsive breakpoints (Phase 10c):** `@media (max-width: 1079.98px)` is the tablet/phone-landscape tier; `@media (max-width: 640px)` is the phone tier; baseline (≥1080px) is desktop. The desktop boundary is 1080px (NOT 1024px) because `.app-shell` requires 360px rail + 720px workbench = 1080px to render without horizontal overflow. iPad Pro portrait at 1024×1366 renders in tablet mode by design.
- **`body { min-width: 1100px }` is gone (Phase 10c).** Do not reintroduce — it forces horizontal scroll on every viewport below 1100px. The responsive grid below handles layout.
- **Mobile-only CSS rules live at the bottom of `frontend/src/styles.css`** in two `@media` blocks. Above the blocks is the desktop baseline; the blocks layer overrides. `body { min-width }` was REMOVED, not gated. The rail's `height: 100vh` is paired with a follow-up `height: 100dvh` declaration so iOS Safari's collapsing URL bar doesn't leave a gap.
- **Tables: the mobile `.table-scroll > table { min-width: 480px }` rule is scoped — NOT a blanket `table` rule.** Three table surfaces exist: (1) tables inside `.table-scroll` (factor shocks, name-level, analogs, theme sensitivity) — the mobile rule applies; (2) markdown-rendered tables inside `.section-content` (methodology drawer) — they get `overflow-x: auto` on the section instead; (3) `.adjustment-history` list — narrow enough to leave alone. If you add a new wide table, wrap it in `<div className="table-scroll">` so it gets the responsive treatment. **(Phase 33)** A fullscreened table card needs the OPPOSITE override: `.fullscreen-surface:fullscreen .table-scroll` / `.fullscreen-surface.is-card-expanded .table-scroll` raise the baseline 420px cap to `calc(100dvh - 200px)` — bounded, not `none`, so the sticky `<th>` still has a scrollport to stick within.
- **Hamburger button class `.rail-toggle-btn` extends `.methodology-btn`** styling for visual consistency. It's `display: none` baseline and switches to `display: inline-flex; order: -1` at `@media (max-width: 1079.98px)`. The button and title share `.topbar-nav-cluster`, so compact/tablet navigation reads as one unit while desktop retains title + status/actions.
- **Touch-target baseline is now 40px** on `.primary-button` / `.ghost-button` / `.segmented button` (was 38px), bumping to 44px at ≤1079.98px. Inputs got `padding: 12px` (was `10px 11px`) baseline + `min-height: 44px` at ≤1079.98px. Slider thumbs are sized to 22×22 globally via `::-webkit-slider-thumb` / `::-moz-range-thumb` — applies to both desktop and mobile for parity.
- **iOS Safari `100dvh`**: `.app-shell` and `.rail` use `min-height: 100vh` followed by `min-height: 100dvh` (declaration-order fallback) so older browsers degrade to `100vh`. The `.drawer-panel` and `.rail-drawer-panel` use `top: 0; bottom: 0` fixed-inset positioning, so no 100vh involved there. `.rail-drawer-panel` and `.drawer-body` add `overscroll-behavior: contain` to prevent rubber-band scroll propagating to the body.
- **Phone-stack pattern for label+icon+input rows.** When a `<label>` contains an icon, text, AND an input (e.g. `.asof-picker label`), the inner content is inline-flex on desktop. On phone you want the icon+text inline on row 1 and the input full-width on row 2 — a flex direction:column on the outer container is NOT enough. Use a 2-column grid on the label with `grid-column: 1 / -1` on the input. See the `@media (max-width: 640px)` block at the bottom of `frontend/src/styles.css`.
- **`.saved-row-head` and `.saved-row-meta` both stack on ≤640px.** Both use `justify-content: space-between` between a left-aligned cell and a right-aligned counterpart; on narrow phones long names would crush the right cell. The phone media-query rule stacks them; the `.saved-open` tap target is bumped to `min-height: 40px` at the same breakpoint since the button is now visually isolated.
- **Phase 12 design system ("Hokusai Deep").** `frontend/src/styles.css` `:root` holds the full palette (`--bg` Prussian indigo, `--accent` sand, `--accent-2` crest-blue, `--up`/`--down`, `--warn`) plus type-scale / radius / shadow / motion / `--focus` / font tokens. The legacy names (`--panel`, `--panel-2`, `--red`, `--green`, `--cyan`, `--amber`) are kept as **aliases** of the new tokens, so every existing `var(--*)` reference re-skinned for free. The alias trick does NOT touch hard-coded literals — new CSS must use tokens, not raw hex/rgba, or it will read as the old theme. CTA (`.primary-button`) is sand-on-ink, NOT red (red is reserved for loss/error). **(Superseded Phase 44:** the palette is ink and indigo, and the CTA is an indigo fill with `--on-accent` text. Red-means-loss is unchanged.**)**
- **`--cyan`/`--amber` are aliases now, not literal colors.** `--cyan → --accent-2` (indigo since Phase 44), `--amber → --warn`. "Active" affordances (segmented/nav/tag) are crest-blue on ink. Don't reintroduce literal `#8bd3ff`/`#f6b84b`.
- **(Phase 44, replaces the Phase 12 ghost glyph + seigaiha crest) The brand mark is the indigo seal.** `.brand-seal` renders 波 in the display face on `--accent` with an `--on-accent` glyph, beside the `nami` wordmark (`panels/RailContent.tsx` `.brand-block`). It is decorative (`aria-hidden`, `pointer-events: none`). The seal is indigo, never vermilion, because red is reserved for losses. `favicon.svg` uses the same seal colours (white seigaiha on indigo). `favicon.ico` and `apple-touch-icon.png` were re-derived from its geometry with a one-shot Pillow round-brush render, not committed; a polyline render showed joint notches at 180px.
- **SSE run-progress is the "wave propagating" signature — per-item, NOT a continuous track.** Each `.run-progress li.active .dot` emits an expanding `::after` ripple (`@keyframes nami-ripple`); done stages settle to a filled `--up` node. The `<ol>` wraps to rows (and goes vertical ≤640px), so do NOT add a single L→R track spanning it — it would cut across wrapped rows.
- **Results "ripple" reveal fires once per mount, not per re-render.** `.results-stack > *` runs `nami-rise` with a per-child stagger. React keeps these nodes across attribution-toggle re-renders, so the animation only plays when a run first lands. Don't key the stack on `attributionMethod` or it will re-animate on every toggle.
- **`prefers-reduced-motion` is handled by one global block** at the bottom of `styles.css` (neutralizes every animation/transition + `scroll-behavior`). `MethodologyDrawer.tsx` ALSO gates its `scrollIntoView` via `scrollBehavior()` (JS can't read the CSS block). New JS-driven smooth scrolls must call `scrollBehavior()` too.
- **Keyboard focus = global `:where(...):focus-visible` ring** (`--focus` double-ring). Inputs keep `outline: none` + a `:focus` border-color; the ring is the keyboard affordance layered on top. Don't re-add bare `outline: none` to buttons without a `:focus-visible` replacement.
- **(Phase 19) `.results-toolbar` is sticky ≥1080px under `--z-sticky` (10)** — the three `max-width` breakpoints are untouched (there are now TWO `min-width` queries: 1080 sticky-toolbar, 1440 wide grid). New sticky elements slot below `--z-backdrop`. The stale-results treatment is a class toggle (`.results-stack.is-stale`) on the SAME nodes so the one-shot `nami-rise` stagger does not replay; `filter: saturate()` creates a stacking context, which is safe because the sticky toolbar and `th` live inside it.
- **(Phase 33) `.fullscreen-surface` is the shared fullscreenable-card class** (both native `:fullscreen`/`::backdrop` and the app-owned `.is-card-expanded` fallback, `position: fixed` at `--z-overlay` — below `--z-toast`). Because `.results-stack.is-stale`'s `filter`/`opacity` (and the `.results-stack > *` `nami-rise` transform) make an ancestor the containing block for `position: fixed` descendants, `html.has-expanded-card` neutralizes them (`filter: none; opacity: 1; animation: none`) so an expanded card fills the viewport instead of rendering bounded/dimmed. Keep those neutralizers in sync if `.is-stale` gains more containing-block-creating properties.
- **(Phase 19, amended Phase 31i 2026-07-12) Wide screens are FULL-BLEED, children are measured:** `--workbench-max` stays `100%`, while `--measure-prose`, `--measure-form`, `--analytics-chart-min`, and `--analytics-table-min` stop copy, authored inputs, and analytical pairs from stretching or crushing. Full-bleed is a shell rule, never permission to remove component-level measures. The rail remains capped at 380px and the old `.results-stack`-level waterfall+exposure grid stays removed.
- **(Phase 40) Surface hierarchy is explicit.** `--elevation-card`, `--elevation-answer`, and `--elevation-overlay` are the only depth tiers; `--shadow` and `--shadow-strong` remain compatibility aliases. Impact + Evidence share one `.results-answer-band` border/background/elevation, and their nested surfaces do not add competing shadows. A completed `.scenario-workspace.has-results` compacts the composer without hiding scenario chips, authored text, as-of date, or run controls.
- **(Phase 23, amended Phase 40) Mobile/zoom conventions from the QA hardening:** (1) grid tracks that can contain tables or long mono strings must be `minmax(0, 1fr)` — NEVER bare `1fr` or an implicit template; a track's `auto` minimum adopts the widest child's min-content and the ≤640px `.table-scroll > table { min-width: 480px }` rule then forces the workbench wider than a phone viewport (`.results-stack` base, `.app-shell`/`.scenario-grid`/`.two-column` compact overrides all carry the guard now, matching the ≥1440 grid). (2) CSS font sizes are rem-only — px font sizes don't respond to browser text-size settings; the 20 legacy px declarations were converted (10px=0.625rem … 14px=0.875rem). (3) The ±1σ idio-band "dispersion floor — not a confidence interval" framing renders as a visible caption under `@media (hover: none)` (`.idio-band-note`) because `title=` tooltips are unreachable on touch. (4) Touch-target additions at ≤1079.98px: `.chip`/`.guide-toggle`/`.drawer-close` 44px, `.chip-copy-btn` 40px (secondary inline affordance), and `.nav-knob input` re-asserted to 44px (its 34px baseline ties the generic input rule on specificity and wins on source order). (5) `.exposure-bar-row` becomes a two-column label/value grid at ≤640px with the track on its own full-width row; labels wrap instead of truncating.
- **(Phase 29) Fonts load from `index.html`** (two preconnects + `<link rel="stylesheet">`), NOT a CSS `@import` — the import serialized the font CSS behind the app stylesheet. Keep the family list in index.html in sync with the `--font-*` tokens in styles.css.
- **(Phase 31, renamed Phase 44) Dual theme: `:root` is the DARK base ("Night"); `:root[data-theme="light"]` ("Paper" — sumi ink on cool off-white, deliberately not cream) overrides EVERY color-bearing token.** The inline boot script in `frontend/index.html` sets `data-theme` before first paint (explicit `nami-theme` localStorage choice wins, else `prefers-color-scheme`), so there is deliberately NO `prefers-color-scheme` branch in CSS — one source of truth. Never hard-code hex/rgba in a rule: a literal renders in ONE theme only. Add a purpose token in `:root` + a light override instead (the "Purpose tints" group is the pattern).
- **(Phase 40) The waterfall reads CSS theme variables directly.** React SVG attributes use `var(--up)`, `var(--down)`, `var(--accent-2)`, `var(--chart-grid)`, `var(--chart-zero)`, and text tokens, so there is no JavaScript color bridge to synchronize. `useTheme()` (`frontend/src/theme.ts`) still owns the global flip: it sets `data-theme`, updates the `theme-color` meta, persists `nami-theme`, and follows OS scheme changes only until the user chooses explicitly. The chart has no independent entrance animation; `.results-stack` remains the single reveal motion.
- **(Phase 31, amended Phase 44) `--accent-ink` is the accent as FOREGROUND** (sortable-header text and indigo text/icons): `#a4c4ee` in dark, `#1f4f8f` in light. The light-theme focus ring now uses `--accent-2`. The rule is unchanged: fills (CTA, slider thumbs) use `--accent`, text uses `--accent-ink`.
- **(Phase 31, amended Phase 32) The ≥1600px wide grid lives INSIDE `.results-tabs .tab-panel`, not on `.results-stack`:** Drivers pairs waterfall + factor table; Positions pairs exposure + name-level table; the position-scoped empty-exposure fallback lets an unpaired table reclaim the row. Don't re-add a `.results-stack`-level 2-track grid — waterfall and exposure are never visible together anymore.
- **(Phase 31) The `--step-*` type scale and `--space-5/6/7` are FLUID clamp()s** (360→1440px viewport interpolation; the 1.25 ratio holds at the wide end). Consumers keep using `var(--step-N)`/`var(--space-N)` unchanged — do NOT add per-breakpoint font-size overrides for anything already on the scale; extend the clamp instead.
- **(Phase 31) The rail track is `clamp(300px, 24vw, 380px)`** — it compresses toward 300px on small desktops (giving the 1080-1439 band a wider workbench) and caps at 380px on wide screens. 300 + 720 = 1020 < 1080, so the drawer-collapse boundary stays 1079.98px; don't shrink the 720px workbench minimum without re-deriving that boundary.
- **(Phase 31i) Adaptive wide composition starts at ≥1600px:** first-run Scenario input/output and the Impact/Evidence answer band pair at 3fr/2fr; completed Scenario results remain full-width. Results tabs retain their chart/table pair but use explicit 620px/440px minimum tracks, and true ultrawides (≥2400px) shift to 7fr/5fr while splitting the composer into authored text + control rail. All tracks containing tables or long text remain `minmax(...)` guarded. **(Phase 34)** `.book-profile-layout` joins this tier at approximately 2fr/3fr for exposure summary vs diagnostics; both tracks keep `min-width: 0`, the table stays in `TableScroll`, and the layout stacks below 1600px.
- **(Phase 31i, amended Phase 34) Phone density is reduced without hiding capability:** access/portfolio chips plus the operations shortcut leave the topbar because the same context/actions exist in the rail drawer; scenario chips sit inside `.scenario-chips-wrap` as one horizontally scrollable, scroll-snapping row with a token-based trailing edge fade and enough right padding that Custom remains reachable; the complete disclaimer remains rendered with tighter type/spacing; run controls stack and the primary action fills the card. The 44px touch-target contract is unchanged.
- **(Phase 34) The phone topbar must shrink and wrap under narrow or enlarged text.** `.topbar-nav-cluster > div { min-width: 0 }` lets the title column surrender space and `.topbar h1 { white-space: normal }` at ≤640px preserves the 320px/200%-text no-page-overflow contract. Horizontal overflow is allowed only inside intentional scrollports such as `.scenario-chips` and `.table-scroll`, never on the document.
- **(Phase 34, amended Phase 35) Result-toolbar groups encode purpose, not just wrapping.** `.results-toolbar-actions` contains Save / Export all / Adjust / saved-only Edit; `.results-toolbar-display` contains value, stressed NAV, units, and reproducibility. Both wrap independently, with display content right-aligned on wider screens and full-width/left-aligned at ≤640px. Compact primary controls remain at least 44px.
- **(Phase 31h) Two tab hierarchy tiers, deliberately distinct:** `.area-tabs` is the PRIMARY nav — a filled pill track (`--sunken` + `--line` border, `width: max-content`) whose active tab is crest-on-ink (`--accent-2` bg + `--ink` text, the segmented convention); `.results-tabs` stays the quieter underline family (0.95rem/600, 3px `--accent-2` underline, NO background wash on active). Don't restyle them back into one look — the sameness was the "flat/unfinished" complaint. **(Phase 44 swapped the looks, keeping them distinct:** area tabs are now large underlined text navigation on the page hairline, and results tabs are a pill track matching the segmented controls, a view switch inside the answer.**)**
- **(Phase 31h) Form fields use `--field` + `--field-line`, NOT `--sunken`.** Dark: `--field: var(--sunken)` (pixel-identical). Light: `#ffffff` / `rgba(20,34,48,0.2)` on `#fdfdfb` cards (Phase 44) — fields must be LIGHTER than the cards around them or they read as tan slabs (the original light-theme complaint). Segmented tracks and ops-meters correctly remain `--sunken`.
- **(Phase 31h, amended Phase 40; superseded Phase 44) The Impact-summary card gradient is gone** — the readout has no card chrome and sits flat on the answer band; direction is carried by the hero figure's colour and sign, not a coloured left edge. Phase 40 replaced the old literal `--shadow` bump with the explicit elevation tiers above; `--line` and `--line-strong` remain the light-theme separation controls. The empty-exposure fallback is `.tab-panel > .table-card:first-child { grid-column: 1 / -1 }` — scoped by POSITION, deliberately not `:not(:has(> .exposure-card))`, which would also match the Drivers panel (it never has an exposure card) and break its waterfall + factor-table pairing. Real bug caught in-browser at 1320px.
- **(Phase 31) Tablet band `@media (min-width: 720px) and (max-width: 1079.98px)` re-pairs `.two-column`** (2-up) between the drawer collapse and the desktop rail; it must stay AFTER the ≤1079.98 block in source order (same specificity — order decides).
- **(Phase 31, amended Phase 35) Copy layering: plain-first labels on the answer layer, quant names one level down.** `frontend/src/copy/glossary.ts` is the single source for plain↔quant term pairs; `copy/InfoTip.tsx` is the tap-friendly ⓘ popover. Key labels include h1 "Portfolio scenario explorer", waterfall "What drove the P&L", Evidence "N historical events · M sources", and KnowYourBook "Free, no LLM". Keep disclaimer, EvidenceBlock honesty captions, "Shock applied"/"P&L contrib", errorCopy, and AdjustmentPanel's keep-or-remove caption unchanged.
